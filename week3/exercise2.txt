The "no shared birthday" probability
It's easier to start here. Imagine the 30 people walk into a room one at a time:

Person 1 can have any birthday: 365/365
Person 2 must avoid person 1's birthday: 364/365
Person 3 must avoid both: 363/365
Person 4: 362/365
...
Person 30: 336/365

Multiply all of those together:
P(no match) = 365/365 × 364/365 × 363/365 × ... × 336/365
Each new person has slightly fewer "safe" birthdays available. You're multiplying 30 fractions that are all just below 1, and small numbers multiplied together many times shrink fast.
That product works out to roughly 0.294, so about 29%.

The "at least one shared birthday" probability
This is just the complement — if there's a 29% chance of no match, then:
P(at least one match) = 1 − 0.294 ≈ 0.706
So roughly 70% — with just 30 people!
